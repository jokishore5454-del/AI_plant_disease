# AGRIVISION AI - PRESENTATION SLIDES CONTENT

## Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Project Title:** AgriVision AI: An Integrated Machine Learning Framework for Crop Disease Detection, Crop Health Monitoring, and Yield Prediction
- **Developers:** Prem Kumar.S (Lead) & Kishore V A (Sub-Developer)
- **Domain:** Artificial Intelligence & Precision Agriculture

---

## Slide 2: Problem Statement & Motivation
- **Challenges:** Crop diseases cause up to 40% of global agricultural yield loss annually.
- **Solution:** Integrated AI platform combining computer vision leaf diagnosis, soil telemetry classification, and gradient boosted harvest forecasting.

---

## Slide 3: Three Core AI Engine Pillars
1. **MobileNetV2 CNN:** Transfer learning visual classification on leaf photographs across 10 target classes.
2. **Random Forest Classifier:** Multi-dimensional soil chemical telemetry (pH, N-P-K, EC) and climate health assessment.
3. **XGBoost Regressor:** Regional harvest yield forecasting based on land area, rainfall, thermal units, and fertilizer rates.

---

## Slide 4: Security & Platform Features
- **JWT & Bcrypt Security:** Industrial-grade encrypted authentication.
- **User Data Isolation:** Strict database partitioning ensuring multi-tenant privacy.
- **Admin Control Panel:** Complete user role management, system audit logging, dataset status, and model telemetry.
- **AI Agronomist Assistant:** Contextual LLM explanations for farmers.
